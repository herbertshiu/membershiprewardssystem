Customer Loyalty Program Software: How to Choose

28 September 2026 · About 7 min read

Customer loyalty program software should make a simple promise measurable: guests return sooner, or spend a little more, without turning every visit into a discount negotiation. Evaluate products by the campaign you will run in the next 30 days, not by a year-two roadmap slide.

Must-have capabilities

  • Configurable earn and burn rules staff can explain in one sentence
  • Digital membership card or wallet pass options
  • Time-boxed coupons that do not stack in confusing ways
  • Segments based on last visit—not only join date
  • Exportable member data and clear ownership terms

Avoid software that only adds another discount channel

If every campaign is “10% off for members,” you are buying a coupon tool. Prefer rewards tied to a specific item, daypart, or return window. For F&B playbooks, see restaurant customer loyalty program.

Metrics that matter in the first quarter

Track members as a share of transactions, median days between visits, and redemption rate. Large sale weeks should be tagged separately so they do not hide weak everyday rules.

Regional note for Singapore operators

If you operate in Singapore, messaging channels, receipt habits, and multi-outlet expectations may differ from Hong Kong defaults. Start with customer loyalty program Singapore after you lock the core earn/burn rule.
